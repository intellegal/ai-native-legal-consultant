# Signal-triggered irreversible-loss checks

Do not run these as an opening questionnaire. Activate only after a user statement or material creates a concrete signal. Ask observable facts first, locate evidence, verify current law/document, give reversible low-lock-in steps, and escalate if uncertain.

| Signal | Plain factual questions | What to protect/check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Old debt or long inactivity | When did the user last demand performance? Did the other side acknowledge, ask for delay, partially perform, give security, or make a plan? Are records preserved? | Dates, complete demand/acknowledgment records; current limitation rules and exceptions. |
| New court/arbitration/agency document | When and how was every page first received, by whom, and what date/action does the document state? What has already been submitted? | Full document and service proof; current response, evidence, appeal/review, enforcement or other procedural rules. |
| Surveillance, chats, web pages, scene, witness, or media may disappear | Who controls it, how long is it retained, can an original/full export be lawfully obtained, and are there independent holders? | Original/metadata/context/acquisition chain; current lawful preservation options. |
| Asset sale, company cancellation, insolvency, multiple creditors | What exact act/source shows the risk? What identifiable assets, accounts, receivables, security, proceedings or public notices exist? | Subject identity, lawful asset clues, current preservation/insolvency options, cost/security risks. |
| Imminent settlement, waiver, resignation, receipt, admission, withdrawal | What full text/attachments must be signed or sent, by when, what is received, and what rights/facts are conceded? | Preserve full draft; verify whether the deadline is real; obtain urgent review before irreversible action. |
| Ongoing transfer, account control, “pay more to unlock” | Is transfer/access ongoing, what was the latest payment, who received it, and have official bank/platform/police channels been contacted? | Stop unverified further loss/access; preserve transactions and communications; seek official/urgent help. |
| Property/injury/product condition will change | Who controls the object, what will repair/return/destroy/treatment change, and what photos, inspection, records, bills, samples or witnesses exist? | Pre-change condition and chain; current appraisal/inspection/preservation needs. |
| Planned or ongoing public dissemination, privacy, secrets | What is public, where, when, how far, what third-party/private/secret content is involved, and what does the user plan next? | Original URL/post and spread; avoid unverified expansion or destroying the sole original; current platform/legal options. |
| Police/regulator inquiry, search, freeze, statement | Which authority, when, in what stated capacity, what written document, what has been said/signed/submitted, and what is next? | Full documents and copies; do not destroy/fabricate/conceal; prompt specialist-lawyer review. |
| Cross-border person, act, law, data, forum or asset | Which element is outside PRC Mainland? Does the contract select law/forum? Is the user asking for PRC or foreign-law analysis? | Split scope, preserve full clauses, identify foreign-law and data-transfer review needs. |
| Notice/renewal/performance window | What does the complete contract say about form/address/time/consequence? What notice/performance occurred and is receipt proven? | Full contract and delivery proof; current rule before termination/non-performance/renewal action. |
| Strong merits but weak recovery | What practical outcome is wanted, what assets/security/insurance/co-obligors are known, and what cost/time can the user bear? | Enforcement and cost reality; compare negotiation/security/staged action and litigation. |

## Priority

- `P0`: direct ongoing danger/loss, same-day compulsory process, request to destroy evidence or evade authorities—interrupt ordinary flow and give concise official/emergency/lawyer escalation.
- `P1`: imminent document signing, evidence loss, asset movement, short procedural window—handle in the current session before broad analysis.
- `P2`: older timing, renewal/performance, appraisal, collection-value issues—resolve before the report.
- `P3`: normal matter analysis.

Never state a fixed legal period from this table. Verify the actual current rule, starting event, document, exceptions, and jurisdiction first.
