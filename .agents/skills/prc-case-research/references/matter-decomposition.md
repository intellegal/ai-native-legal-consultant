# Matter-Level Research Planning

Use this guide only when the incoming request is broader than one clear case-research question. Convert the matter into a concise, user-confirmed research scope before external case retrieval. When the skill is hosted by `$voice-legal-consultation-cn`, reuse the canonical matter workpaper and let the host own the spoken confirmation; do not restart intake or add a second approval turn.

## Build from the Decision

1. State the practical decision the user needs to make, such as whether to sue, which claim or defense to use, what evidence to obtain, whether to settle, or which procedural route to take. Separate that objective from any preferred outcome.
2. Organize only the facts needed to understand that decision. Distinguish established or documented facts, party allegations, disputed facts, missing facts, and material dates. Use a timeline or party map only when it improves comprehension.
3. Identify provisional legal routes without settling them prematurely. Ask what must be true for each plausible claim, defense, procedural path, remedy, or risk conclusion to hold.
4. Follow those dependencies until reaching facts, legal rules, judicial applications, evidence questions, procedural requirements, or professional questions that can be checked.
5. Retain only unresolved nodes whose answers could materially change the user's decision, a controlling legal route, the evidence plan, the procedural path, or the value of later research. Merge overlapping questions and retire branches that are immaterial on the known facts.
6. After deriving the questions, use actors and relationships, event stages, procedure, facts and evidence, legal meaning, causation, responsibility, remedies, and enforceability only as optional omission prompts. Do not force every matter through a preset taxonomy.

## Route Each Question

Route a question to case research when all four considerations are materially present:

- **Materiality**: the answer can change a claim, defense, evidence action, procedure, remedy, negotiation position, or research branch;
- **Unresolved status**: the available facts and current authoritative rules do not answer it at the level the task requires;
- **Adjudicative dependence**: how courts operationalize a rule against similar facts, evidence, procedure, responsibility, or remedy is part of the answer;
- **Comparability**: cases can be compared on controlling dimensions rather than by superficial topic similarity.

Do not use case retrieval as the default for every unresolved question:

- use current statutes, judicial interpretations, binding rules, or other authoritative sources for current-law questions;
- request evidence or user clarification for historical or case-specific factual gaps;
- use current official court forms, guides, or rules for local filing and operating requirements;
- use appraisal, audit, medical, technical, or other professional sources for questions that require expertise;
- use cases only as analogical or exploratory material when novelty or weak comparability prevents a stable practice conclusion, and disclose that limit.

Finding cases can verify that a judicial approach exists or show how courts reason under comparable conditions. It does not by itself prove prevalence, uniformity, or the truth of the user's facts.

## Produce the Human-Review Plan

Create one concise Markdown document named `003_案件梳理与案例检索建议.md`. In a hosted consultation, place it under `003_内部工作区/`. Prefer clear prose and short numbered questions over cards, dense tables, or internal schemas. Include only:

1. **研究目标与边界**: the user's practical decision, the intended depth, and material assumptions;
2. **案情整理**: the relevant parties, events, documents, established facts, disputed facts, missing facts, and material dates;
3. **暂定分析路径**: plausible legal relationships, claims, defenses, procedural routes, or competing characterizations, clearly labeled as provisional;
4. **建议进一步研究的关键问题**: for each proposed question, state the neutral question, why it can change the matter, and the recommended source in a short paragraph;
5. **不建议进行案例检索的问题**: identify issues better answered by current law, official court materials, evidence, clarification, or professional expertise;
6. **拟定检索顺序与范围**: explain dependencies, priority, material scope, and exclusions without exposing the full keyword design;
7. **请用户确认**: ask the user to correct the facts, add, remove, merge, or reprioritize questions, and confirm whether retrieval should begin.

Do not begin external case calls until confirmation unless the user explicitly waives the gate. In a hosted consultation, return the plan to the voice host, which gives one short spoken scope summary and records the user's confirmation in the canonical workpaper. A limited check of user-provided materials, verified local sources, or current authoritative law is allowed when necessary to frame the plan; label any resulting view as preliminary.

## Execute and Reopen Scope

After confirmation, convert the approved questions into internal research units and use the normal adaptive retrieval loop. Do not require the user to approve keywords, synonyms, result depth, full-text pulls, or ordinary contrary and gap-filling moves.

Update the plan and request confirmation again only when a discovery materially expands cost or scope, changes the controlling legal route or user decision, or creates a new issue that cannot reasonably fit within an approved question. In the final report, synthesize interactions across the approved questions rather than presenting isolated research answers.
