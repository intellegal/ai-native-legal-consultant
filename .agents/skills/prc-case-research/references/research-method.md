# Progressive Case Research

This is the shared research method. Source modules choose executable queries; the context profile chooses output placement. Neither changes the evidence standard.

## Goal and scope

- Identify the user's decision, material unresolved questions, confirmed scope and source choice. Infer only what is necessary and label it. Reuse supplied cases and workpapers as evidence, seeds or controls as appropriate.
- For a whole matter, use `matter-decomposition.md` to organize dependencies and route factual, current-law, official-procedure and professional questions to suitable sources. Record the plan in the research record; a separate planning document is optional.
- Reuse authorization already present in the task or host. Clarify material ambiguity or a genuinely new matter outside the request, not each query, added case, synonym or ordinary change of reasoning within scope.

## Learn vocabulary through cases

Initial expressions are working hypotheses. Retrieve relevant cases early enough to learn judicial language before committing to a detailed keyword design. The initial amount and mix depend on the task and actual results; there is no mandatory one-or-two-probe sequence.

Alternate retrieval, candidate screening, full-text reading, comparison, vocabulary extraction and query redesign throughout the project. Do not execute an initial keyword list unchanged to completion. Use `keyword-matrix.md` for query intent and only the selected provider for syntax.

For each controlling issue, test the user's strongest application and plausible opposing or distinguishing explanations. Choose useful fact, evidence, procedure, remedy, authority, time or regional dimensions; do not force a preset taxonomy or universal relevance score. Use several materially different paths when justified, and refine an untested or noisy path when that is more informative.

## Coverage and full-text selection

Keep candidate breadth, original texts acquired, texts saved, texts closely read, verified conclusions, independent judgment documents and independent disputes distinct. Tool hit totals and requested batch sizes are different again. Saving a file does not prove it has been read or verified.

Map decision-relevant gaps dynamically. An explicit coverage table helps complex matters, empirical work, consultation and omission checking; a narrow question may record the same reasoning briefly. High overlap across queries is a reason to inspect lexical/ranking bias and missing fact types, not proof of saturation.

Read next the text most likely to fill a gap, resolve a reasoning or identity uncertainty, reveal useful vocabulary, provide a clearer merits holding, or test a lower-ranked/semantic-only/underrepresented path. Repeated examples are useful when the task's empirical design calls for them. There is no default, minimum, target or total ceiling for candidates, paths or full texts.

Major case conclusions and statistics about judicial reasoning require original-text verification. Preserve the complete judgment text for every closely read case and every case cited as a judgment in the report; link the local file under `output-schema.md`. A summary, case note, snippet or processed semantic result cannot satisfy that requirement. If the original is unavailable, record the lead and gap without presenting it as a verified judgment citation.

When comparisons, duplicate relationships, empirical independence or known omissions matter, read `fulltext-comparison-and-deduplication.md`. Metadata is provisional identity evidence, not a final reason to merge or exclude. Use known-case blind and diagnostic recovery controls when available.

## Decide the next move

Choose from evidence: tighten repeatable noise; expand learned expressions; test a competing characterization or burden; fill a factual/procedural/remedial/authority gap; deepen a productive path; revisit a possibly misleading filter; or close a saturated path. Apply this symmetrically to favorable and adverse material.

Use semantic or official-source discovery when relevant and available; a provider is not required to supply unsupported semantic retrieval. Honor the specified source within the capabilities supported by the installed edition; a material source substitution requires the user's choice and an actually supported provider rather than a silent fallback. A substitute query can be non-equivalent: record its changed meaning, compensating verification and residual gap.

## Quality acceptance and stopping

Continue while the research purpose is not sufficiently met, a material alternative remains untested, the case set is too narrow for the question, or another feasible round is likely to materially improve coverage or the conclusion. Neither a number reached nor a tool's single-response cap is an acceptance test. More records alone are not a reason to continue after useful improvement has ceased.

Finish as research-complete only when the task-relevant questions and plausible alternatives have been addressed, major claims are traceable to locally retained verified texts, comparisons and identity relationships are resolved to the required level, and remaining uncertainty is immaterial or explicitly qualifies the conclusion. For empirical claims, explain sampling units, design and limits rather than offering a universal minimum or a win-rate guess.

If actual credits are exhausted or insufficient for further retrieval, stop further retrieval, preserve current conclusions/full texts/the research record, identify completed and unresolved work, accurately explain the balance condition and ask the user how to proceed. An unconfirmed network or tool error is not evidence that credits are exhausted. Do not silently switch providers, reduce the requested quality, retry chargeable calls, purchase credits or resume without the user's decision. Keep other tool/access interruptions distinct from research saturation.

There is no built-in POINT allowance, spending target or mandatory pricing checkpoint. Respect any limit the user actually supplies. Source metadata and prices are operational facts, not a way to decide the research scope. A documentation check can help diagnose a real pricing uncertainty without becoming a routine approval gate.

## Synthesis

Lead with a qualified answer. Explain the decisive original reasoning, controlling similarities and differences, strongest adverse evidence/argument, sensitive unknowns and practical implications. Keep current rules, case observations, practical inferences and unresolved verification distinct. Deadlines and local filing requirements need current authoritative support. Use `practical-analysis.md` when evidence, procedure or action decisions matter.

Maintain the concise report, one research record and the required local judgment files as described in `output-schema.md`; supporting datasets and separate indexes are optional, task-driven additions.
