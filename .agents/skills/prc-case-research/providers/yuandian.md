# YuanDian MCP Strategy

Use when the user selects YuanDian or leaves the case database unspecified. Discover the available `mcp__yuandian_case` tools; if absent, look for the configured YuanDian case capability. Do not silently substitute another source when it is unavailable.

Read `../references/yuandian-optimization.md` for the single maintained source of syntax, metadata, price observations and transport limits. This file specifies how to use those capabilities for research, not duplicate operational constants.

- Choose ordinary/authoritative keyword, semantic discovery and detail retrieval by the uncertainty being addressed. Source labels are not proof of independent disputes or of a complete judgment body.
- Design expressions for supported fields and global connectors. For an unsupported grouped intent, split meaningful branches and reconcile the results. For unavailable NOT or proximity, use supported recall and inspect the original context; document non-equivalence and remaining omissions instead of claiming parser equivalence.
- Determine per-call sizes from noise, useful breadth and what the bridge can return. A per-call keyword maximum does not cap the whole task. Expand through useful expressions or meaningful date/court/issue partitions; do not just repeat the same ranked head and call it new coverage.
- Large local caching cannot rescue a response rejected upstream. On overflow, record the failed capture, use a smaller successful batch or a justified partition, and keep the research goal unchanged.
- Use semantic discovery for vocabulary or coverage gaps. Inspect its incremental cases against keyword results, then verify original judgments. Set query rewriting deliberately for exploration versus controlled calibration rather than relying on an assumed default.
- Prefer reasoning fields for judicial treatment and full text for facts, evidence and submissions. Use scope filters supplied by the user immediately; calibrate additional noise filters and check their exclusions.
- Retrieve and retain the full judgments selected for close reading or citation, with reusable identifiers and metadata captured at first success. Do not fetch details only to recover metadata already available.
- Continue and accept the result under the shared method, without a provider-specific total case count or preset credit allowance. Confirm actual credit exhaustion/insufficiency from a reliable response before invoking the shared stop-and-ask rule.
